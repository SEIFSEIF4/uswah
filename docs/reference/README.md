# Reference

Material Uswah is designed against. Nothing here is ours; it is kept for looking at.

| Folder | What it is | Why it is here |
|---|---|---|
| `nawiya/` | Seif's earlier project, built around niyyah (intention) | A feature Uswah may absorb. Read `nawiya.md` first. |
| `qalam/` | qalam.global, a history publication on the Turkic world | The layout reference: hero bands, category eyebrows, read times, feature strips |
| `thmanyah/` | thmanyah.com, Saudi media, Arabic-first | The RTL reference, and the source of our Arabic typeface |
| `motqen/` | متقن, a Quran-memorisation association | Design spec screenshots showing Arabic nav patterns and programme cards |
| `unsorted/` | Screenshots I could not confidently attribute | Sorted by hand when someone recognises them |

`unsorted/` is honest rather than tidy. These arrived in one batch with the others and I
did not open every one, so rather than guess at labels they are parked with their original
sequence intact.

## Qalam pages worth opening directly

- <https://qalam.global/en/section/Features/all> — the section index, and the pattern our
  topic pages follow
- <https://tests.qalam.global/en> — their quiz format

`qalam/qalam-article-contents.png` shows the sticky contents panel on a long read. Ours are
one to three minutes, so we do not need it, but the treatment of a long index as a designed
object rather than a sidebar is worth stealing later if article lengths grow.

## A note on copying

Layout patterns are common property and we use them freely. Wordmarks, logos, signature
colours and illustration styles are not. Uswah gets its own.
